Dr. Jitendra Narayan is a Senior Scientist at CSIR-Institute of Genomics and Integrative Biology (CSIR-IGIB), New Delhi, India, where he leads **JitendraLab**, a research group working at the intersection of computational genomics, bioinformatics, and artificial intelligence for life sciences.

His research focuses on pathogen genomics, antimicrobial resistance (AMR), comparative genomics, microbial evolution, metagenomics, infectious disease surveillance, and AI-driven biological data analysis. He has developed open-source bioinformatics tools, computational pipelines, and genomic resources supporting research and public health.

Dr. Narayan holds a Ph.D. in Bioinformatics from Aberystwyth University, UK, and conducted postdoctoral research at the University of Namur, Belgium. He has contributed to national and international projects in genomic epidemiology, One Health, wastewater surveillance, and emerging infectious diseases.

He is also the Founder of BOL (Bioinformatics Online), India, an initiative dedicated to advancing bioinformatics education, training, and computational biology resources: [https//:bioinformaticsonline.com.](https://www.bioinformaticsonline.com/)

He is passionate about mentoring young scientists, promoting open science, and leveraging AI and genomics to accelerate biological discovery and improve global health.
