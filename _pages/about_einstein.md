Dr. Jitendra Narayan is a Senior Scientist at the CSIR-Institute of Genomics and Integrative Biology (CSIR-IGIB), New Delhi, India, where he leads **JitendraLab**, a research group focused on computational genomics, bioinformatics, and artificial intelligence for life sciences. His research integrates large-scale genomic data analysis with machine learning to understand pathogen evolution, antimicrobial resistance (AMR), comparative genomics, and infectious disease surveillance.

He received his Ph.D. in Bioinformatics from Aberystwyth University, United Kingdom, followed by postdoctoral research at the University of Namur, Belgium. Throughout his career, he has developed numerous open-source bioinformatics tools, computational pipelines, and genomic databases that support researchers and public health agencies worldwide.

The primary research interests of JitendraLab include comparative genomics, pathogen genomics, antimicrobial resistance, microbial evolution, metagenomics, structural variation analysis, horizontal gene transfer, genome informatics, and AI-driven biological data analysis. His group develops scalable computational frameworks for genomic surveillance and precision public health, translating complex biological data into actionable insights.

Dr. Narayan has led and contributed to several national and international collaborative projects involving genomic epidemiology, wastewater surveillance, One Health, and emerging infectious diseases. His laboratory has developed widely used bioinformatics resources, including platforms for AMR surveillance, pathogen genome analysis, biomarker discovery, and comparative genomics.

Beyond research, he is committed to mentoring young scientists and promoting open science through workshops, training programs, and freely available computational resources. His vision is to bridge artificial intelligence and genomics to accelerate biological discovery and improve global health.
